3. Layout

Preview of the banner: 9 skins per row like NameMC. The top-left slot is your current skin and stays as it is, so the banner starts one slot later.

4. Your 26 skins

Choose an image to generate the skins.

Click a skin to download it. NameMC lists the newest skin first, so skins are applied last-tile-first. Your own skin goes back on at the end so it stays in the first slot.

Open NameMC

Manual: after each skin, open NameMC and refresh your profile so it records that skin, then apply the next one.

Automatic: applies all 26 in order, then puts your skin back. Keep your NameMC profile open in another tab and refresh it while this runs. NameMC only records skins it sees, so a longer delay is safer.

Setup: put your Azure app client ID in CONFIG at the top of the script, and your Supabase URL and anon key in SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_KEY if you want the mailing list. Xbox and Minecraft services block browser requests from other sites, so set PROXY to your own CORS proxy.
